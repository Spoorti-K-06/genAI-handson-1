This project benchmarks BERT, RoBERTa, and BART on Generation, Fill-Mask, and Question Answering tasks. BERT and RoBERTa fail at text generation since they are encoder-only models not trained for next-token prediction. All models perform well on the Fill-Mask task, with BERT and RoBERTa giving the most accurate results. For Question Answering, BERT and RoBERTa successfully extract correct answers from the given context. BART performs best in text generation due to its encoder–decoder (seq2seq) architecture.
